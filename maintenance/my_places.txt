# CampusGo: our own places, added to search on top of OpenStreetMap.
#
# Use it for societies, colonies and spots riders ask for that OpenStreetMap doesn't have.
# One place per line:   Name | Town | latitude, longitude
#
# To get the numbers: open Google Maps, long-press the society's main gate (phone) or
# right-click it (laptop), and copy the two numbers it shows, e.g. 30.73291, 76.67532.
# Lines starting with # are ignored, so a line stays switched off until you remove its #.
# After editing, run:  python maintenance/build_places.py

# Lines without a # are live. Pins without a Google Maps check may be a little off; riders can drag the
# pin to the exact gate, and if you check one on Google Maps, replace its numbers.
