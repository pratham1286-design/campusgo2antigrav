Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

CampusGo is operated by [OPERATOR NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the CampusGo app and website, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, username, LPU ID (registration number or faculty ID), mobile number, and whether you are a student or a teacher. Your number is verified with a one-time SMS code.
  • Location: your device's precise location while CampusGo is open, to set your pickup and show nearby rides. If you drive, your phone sends its position about every 15 seconds while you are online or on a trip; this is how riders are matched to you and how a pickup and drop-off are confirmed.
  • Trips: pickup and drop-off places, times, fares, the start PIN, and ratings and comments.
  • Vehicle (drivers): vehicle type, model, plate number, colour and seats.
  • Emergency contacts: the names, relationships and phone numbers you add. Only add people who agree to receive an SOS text from CampusGo.
  • Wallet and payments: your balance, top-ups, fares, refunds and, for drivers, payout requests and the UPI ID you want to be paid to. Card, UPI and bank details you enter in Razorpay Checkout go to Razorpay, not to us; we receive the payment's reference and status.
  • SOS alerts: the time, your location and which contacts were texted.
  • Technical data: your IP address (to stop abuse such as repeated sign-in attempts) and a sign-in token stored on your device.

We do not show ads, sell your data, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Who can see what

  • During a ride, you and your driver or rider see each other's name, phone number and the vehicle, and the rider sees the driver's live position.
  • Carpools: everyone signed in can see open carpools with the host's and passengers' first names, the route, time, vehicle model and plate. The host sees passengers' names and phone numbers; passengers see the host's.
  • Available drivers appear on other users' maps by vehicle type only, without names, and with their position rounded to about 100 metres.
  • Trip links you share: anyone with the link sees first names, the vehicle and its live position until the trip ends.
  • SOS: your emergency contacts receive a text with your name, LPU ID and your location or a live trip link.

Service providers

We use these companies to run CampusGo. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • [HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g. Render] (servers and database);
  • Fast2SMS or Twilio (sign-in codes and SOS texts);
  • Razorpay (payments);
  • OpenStreetMap or [MAP TILE PROVIDER] (map images: your device downloads them directly, so the provider sees your IP address and the map area you view). Place search and routes are fetched by our server, not by your device;
  • Google Fonts (the app's typefaces; Google receives your IP address).

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you use CampusGo. Sign-in codes are deleted within about an hour, and a driver's position is overwritten with each update.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile → Delete Account. You can also email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the number on your account and we will delete it for you.

Deleting removes your name, username, LPU ID, phone number, emergency contacts, vehicle and location at once, and signs you out everywhere. Records of past trips, payments, payouts and SOS alerts are kept without your name or number for as long as the law requires (for accounting, tax, fraud prevention and safety investigations). Any wallet balance is lost, so ask us for a refund before you delete.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to access, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and complain to us or to the Data Protection Board of India. Contact our grievance officer: [GRIEVANCE OFFICER NAME], [CONTACT EMAIL].

Age

CampusGo is for LPU students and staff aged 18 or over.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in codes are stored only as keyed hashes, sessions use signed tokens that end when you log out, and every request is checked against the signed-in account.
