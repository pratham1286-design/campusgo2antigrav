Who can use CampusGo

CampusGo is for students and staff of Lovely Professional University aged 18 or over. Use your own name, LPU ID and mobile number, keep one account, and keep your phone secure: anyone who can read your SMS codes can sign in as you.

What CampusGo does

CampusGo connects riders with members of the LPU community who offer rides on their own bike, scooty or car, and lets drivers offer seats on scheduled carpools. Drivers choose which requests to accept.

Fares and fees

  • Campus Hop (on-campus trips): a flat ₹14. The driver receives ₹13 and CampusGo keeps ₹1.
  • CityLink (trips with an off-campus end): a base fare plus a per-kilometre rate, multiplied by up to 1.5× at class-change rush hours and busy times. The price is shown before you book. The driver receives 90% and CampusGo keeps 10%.
  • Carpools: the host sets the price per seat. The host receives 90% and CampusGo keeps 10%.

Wallet

  • Rides are paid from your CampusGo wallet, which can only be used for CampusGo trips and earns no interest.
  • Top-ups through Razorpay are added as soon as the payment succeeds. A direct UPI payment is added after we match it with our bank statement.
  • Money you top up cannot be withdrawn. Ask us at pratham1286@gmail.com if you need a refund of an unused balance.
  • Drivers can withdraw what they earned from trips to a UPI ID. We send the money and mark the request paid.

Booking, cancelling and refunds

  • The fare is held from your wallet when you book. If you cancel before the trip starts, it is refunded in full. A request no driver accepts within 30 minutes is cancelled and refunded automatically.
  • A ride starts only when the driver enters the 4-digit PIN shown on the rider's phone, so give your PIN only once you are with your driver.
  • Carpool passengers can leave before departure for a full refund. If the host cancels, or does not start the trip within 6 hours of the departure time, every passenger is refunded.
  • A trip that was started but never marked complete is completed and paid automatically after 12 hours.

You are responsible for holding a valid driving licence, registration, insurance and any permit the law requires for carrying passengers, for following traffic law, for carrying a helmet for a two-wheeler passenger, and for never driving under the influence. Keep location turned on while online or on a trip; positions that are faked or impossible are rejected.
